About

Computer enthusiast, cybersecurity graduate and practical builder.

My background sits between formal cyber security study and hands-on technical projects. I like working through how systems are put together, why they fail, and how to make them easier to use or maintain.

Background

I graduated from Buckinghamshire New University with a BSc in Cyber Security, achieving Upper Second-Class Honours with an overall result of 69%. My degree covered network systems, data management, malware, databases, online activity risks and cyber security threats.

Before university, I studied T-Level Digital Production, Design and Development, Games Design Level 2, IT and Computer Science. That gave me a practical base in Python, C#, SQL, software design, digital design and problem solving.

Outside coursework, I have built and maintained my own projects: a Nextcloud server, a Jellyfin media server, websites, a full desktop PC build, and a cross-platform Python diagnostic toolkit.

How I work

Start with the real problem.

I prefer practical tasks where the goal is clear: make a service reachable, diagnose a connection issue, improve a website or document a setup so it can be repeated.

Use tools carefully.

My projects have involved Docker, Tailscale, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL, MongoDB basics, Linux, Windows and Android Termux. I try to understand the trade-offs instead of just copying commands.

Keep learning visible.

I like projects that produce something testable: a report, a hosted service, a working site, a local tool or a documented workflow.

Personal interests

I have a long-running interest in computers, gaming, home servers, custom PCs, technical problem solving and cats.

Languages

Romanian, English, Spanish and Portuguese. Portuguese is included because I am actively learning it for relocation and work in Portugal.
